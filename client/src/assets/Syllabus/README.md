# 🇩🇪 German B1 Roadmap (A0 → B1)

> A structured six-month German curriculum designed to take an absolute beginner from **CEFR A0 to Goethe/TELC B1** through consistent daily practice across all core language skills.

---

# Vision

Learning German is not about memorizing vocabulary or completing Duolingo streaks.

It is about developing the ability to **understand**, **communicate**, **read**, **write**, and **think** in German.

This roadmap combines internationally recognized CEFR competencies with structured daily practice and exam-oriented preparation.

Rather than following random YouTube videos or disconnected grammar lessons, every topic has been carefully sequenced to ensure each concept builds upon previously acquired knowledge.

The roadmap emphasizes consistency over intensity:

- 3 hours every day
- 26 structured weeks
- 182 study days
- Continuous revision
- Progressive exam preparation

---

# Primary Goal

Reach a level where you can confidently:

- Pass Goethe B1
- Pass TELC B1
- Communicate during everyday situations in Germany
- Prepare for Ausbildung interviews
- Read beginner and intermediate German texts
- Write emails and formal letters
- Hold conversations with native speakers

# Vocabulary Strategy

Every day includes

- 15 new words
- pronunciation
- gender
- plural forms
- verb forms (when applicable)
- example sentence
- common collocations
- spaced repetition review

Vocabulary is organized by real-life topics rather than alphabetical lists.

Examples

- Family
- Shopping
- Restaurant
- School
- Work
- Health
- Housing
- Banking
- Transportation
- Technology

---

# Grammar Strategy

Grammar follows CEFR progression instead of textbook order.

Topics are introduced only when prerequisite concepts have already been mastered.

The curriculum gradually progresses through

- Articles
- Pronouns
- Present tense
- Modal verbs
- Cases
- Adjectives
- Separable verbs
- Perfect tense
- Word order
- Subordinate clauses
- Relative clauses
- Passive voice
- Konjunktiv II

---

# Reading Strategy

Reading starts with

- signs
- menus
- advertisements

then gradually progresses to

- short stories
- emails
- news
- blogs
- official letters

Every reading lesson includes comprehension questions.

---

# Listening Strategy

Listening materials gradually increase in difficulty.

Primary resources include

- Deutsche Welle
- Easy German
- Slow German Podcast

Each listening exercise includes

- comprehension questions
- vocabulary extraction
- pronunciation practice

---

# Speaking Strategy

Speaking begins on Day 1.

Daily activities include

- repetition
- shadowing
- pronunciation
- guided conversations
- role play
- picture description
- opinion speaking

Speaking confidence is built gradually through structured practice.

---

# Writing Strategy

Writing evolves alongside grammar.

Examples include

A1

- Fill forms
- Greetings
- Short messages

A2

- Invitations
- Emails
- Daily diary

B1

- Opinion essays
- Complaint letters
- Formal emails
- Experience reports

Every writing task includes a checklist for self-review.

---

# Revision Philosophy

Revision happens every day.

Instead of learning vocabulary once,

the roadmap uses spaced repetition.

Review schedule

- Yesterday
- 3 days ago
- 7 days ago
- 14 days ago
- 30 days ago

Grammar and listening are also revisited regularly.

---

# Weekly Assessments

Every Sunday includes

- Grammar Quiz
- Vocabulary Test
- Reading Test
- Listening Test
- Speaking Task
- Writing Assignment

Weekly assessments measure progress before introducing new material.

---

# Monthly Mock Exams

At the end of every four weeks

Each mock exam evaluates

- Reading

- Writing
- Speaking
- Grammar
- Vocabulary
