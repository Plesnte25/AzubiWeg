# 🗣 Week 02 Speaking Assessment

> **Goal:** Demonstrate your ability to talk about your family, people, and everyday objects using the grammar and vocabulary learned during Week 02.

Speaking assessment focuses on:

- Pronunciation
- Vocabulary usage
- Sentence formation
- Confidence
- Ability to communicate basic ideas

Perfect grammar is not required. The goal is understandable communication.

---

# 🎯 Assessment Overview

| Task | Skill Tested | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Task 1 | Self Introduction Revision | 2 min |
| Task 2 | Family Description | 3 min |
| Task 3 | Object Description | 2 min |
| Task 4 | Question & Answer | 3 min |
| Task 5 | Free Speaking | 3 min |

---

# 🗣 Task 1 — Introduce Yourself

## Instruction

Introduce yourself naturally.

Include:

- Name
- Country
- City
- Language
- Family (briefly)

---

## Example Structure

> Hallo!  
> Ich heiße ________.  
> Ich komme aus ________.  
> Ich wohne in ________.  
> Ich habe eine Familie.  
> Meine Familie ist ________.

Do not memorize this exact example.

Create your own version.

---

# 🗣 Task 2 — Talk About Your Family

## Instruction

Speak for approximately 3 minutes.

Describe:

- Parents
- Brothers/Sisters
- Family members
- Occupations
- Where they live

---

## Required Grammar

Try to use:

- mein
- meine
- dein
- der
- die
- das
- sein

---

## Useful Questions

Answer these aloud:

### Wer ist das?

(Who is that?)

---

### Wer ist dein Vater?

(Who is your father?)

---

### Hast du Geschwister?

(Do you have siblings?)

---

### Was macht deine Mutter?

(What does your mother do?)

---

# 🗣 Task 3 — Describe Objects Around You

## Instruction

Choose five objects near you.

Describe them in German.

Examples:

- Book
- Table
- Chair
- Phone
- Laptop

---

## Sentence Pattern

```
Das ist + article + noun.
```

Examples:

Das ist ein Buch.

Das ist ein Tisch.

Das ist meine Tasche.

---

## Challenge

Add descriptions:

Examples:

- Das Buch ist blau.
- Der Tisch ist groß.
- Die Tasche ist neu.
