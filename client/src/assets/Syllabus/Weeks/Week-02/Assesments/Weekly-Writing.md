# ✍ Week 02 Writing Assessment

## Objective

This week's writing task evaluates your ability to:

- Describe your family.
- Use **der/die/das** correctly.
- Use **mein/meine** and **dein/deine**.
- Create simple descriptive sentences.
- Apply Week 02 vocabulary naturally.

---

# 📝 Task 1 — Describe Your Family

Write a short paragraph about your family.

**Length: 60–80 words**

Include:

- Your family members.
- Their names.
- Their occupations.
- Where they live.
- Simple descriptions.

---

## Suggested Structure

### Introduction

Example:

> Das ist meine Familie.

---

### Family Members

Include:

- Vater
- Mutter
- Bruder / Schwester
- Other relatives (optional)

---

### Information

Include:

- Name
- Job
- City
- Simple characteristics

# 📝 Task 2 — Describe Objects Around You

Write **8–10 sentences** describing objects in your room.

Use:

- Articles (**der, die, das**)
- Possessive pronouns (**mein/meine**)
- Colors
- Simple adjectives

---

## Example Structure

> Das ist mein Tisch.  
> Der Tisch ist braun.  
> Das ist meine Tasche.  
> Die Tasche ist schwarz.
