# 🟥 Week 02 Grammar

> **Theme:** *Articles, Gender & Possession*

This week introduces one of the most important concepts in German: **every noun has a grammatical gender**. Learning nouns together with their articles from the beginning will make future grammar much easier.

---

# 📚 Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Use **der**, **die**, and **das** correctly with common nouns.
- Distinguish between definite and indefinite articles.
- Identify masculine, feminine, and neuter nouns.
- Form simple plural nouns.
- Use possessive pronouns (**mein**, **dein**).
- Describe people and everyday objects using simple adjectives.
- Build short descriptive sentences.

# 💡 Grammar Tips

- Always learn nouns with their article.
- Do not translate gender from English.
- Focus on communication first, perfection later.
- Read every example aloud to reinforce pronunciation.

---

# ⚠ Common Beginner Mistakes

Avoid:

- Guessing noun gender.
- Memorizing nouns without articles.
- Mixing **ein** and **eine**.
- Forgetting to use possessive pronouns correctly.
- Ignoring plural forms.
