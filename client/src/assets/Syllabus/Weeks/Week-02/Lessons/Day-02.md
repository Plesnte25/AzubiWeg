# 🟥 Grammar Lesson

# What is Noun Gender?

In German, every noun has a grammatical gender.

There are three categories:

| Gender | Article | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Masculine | der | der Vater |
| Feminine | die | die Mutter |
| Neuter | das | das Kind |

---

# Important Rule

Never learn:

❌ Vater = Father

Learn:

✅ der Vater = the father

Because the article tells you the noun's gender.

---

# 1. Masculine Nouns (der)

Common masculine nouns often refer to:

- Male people
- Days
- Months
- Seasons
- Many weather terms

---

## Examples

| German | English |
|---|---|
| der Vater | father |
| der Bruder | brother |
| der Mann | man |
| der Sohn | son |
| der Freund | friend |
| der Lehrer | teacher |

---

# 2. Feminine Nouns (die)

Common feminine nouns often include:

- Female people
- Many nouns ending in certain suffixes

---

## Examples

| German | English |
|---|---|
| die Mutter | mother |
| die Schwester | sister |
| die Frau | woman |
| die Tochter | daughter |
| die Freundin | female friend |
| die Lehrerin | female teacher |

---

# 3. Neuter Nouns (das)

Common neuter nouns often include:

- Young people
- Objects
- Many nouns ending with specific suffixes

---

## Examples

| German | English |
|---|---|
| das Kind | child |
| das Baby | baby |
| das Buch | book |
| das Haus | house |
| das Zimmer | room |
| das Bild | picture |

---

# 🧠 Gender Learning Strategy

Do not memorize:

```
Mutter
```

Memorize:

```
die Mutter
```

Do not memorize:

```
Buch
```

Memorize:

```
das Buch
```

Your brain should automatically connect:

Word → Article → Meaning

---

# ⚠️ Common Beginner Mistakes

## Mistake 1

Learning only the English meaning.

❌ Tisch = table

Correct:

✅ der Tisch = table

---

## Mistake 2

Changing articles randomly.

❌ die Vater

Correct:

✅ der Vater

---

## Mistake 3

Assuming biological gender always works.

Example:

das Mädchen

means:

the girl

Even though it refers to a female person, grammatically it is neuter.

---

# 📌 Gender Patterns (Beginner Level)

These are not 100% rules, but useful patterns.

---

## Usually Masculine (der)

Words ending with:

- -er
- -ling
- -ismus

Examples:

der Lehrer

der Frühling

der Tourismus

---

## Usually Feminine (die)

Words ending with:

- -ung
- -heit
- -keit
- -schaft

Examples:

die Wohnung

die Gesundheit

die Möglichkeit

die Freundschaft

---

## Usually Neuter (das)

Words ending with:

- -chen
- -lein

Examples:

das Mädchen

das Büchlein
