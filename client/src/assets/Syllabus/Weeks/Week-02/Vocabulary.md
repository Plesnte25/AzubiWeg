# 📖 Week 02 Vocabulary

> **Purpose:** This document outlines the vocabulary focus for Week 02. All detailed vocabulary entries, pronunciations, examples, and revisions are managed in your **Obsidian Vocabulary Vault**. This roadmap defines *what* to learn and *how* to practice it throughout the week.

---

# 🎯 Weekly Vocabulary Goal

- **Target:** 105 new words
- **Daily Goal:** 15 new words
- **Daily Revision:** Review previously learned vocabulary using spaced repetition.

The objective is to begin describing **people, family, and everyday objects** using complete German sentences.

---

# 📚 Vocabulary Themes

During Week 02, your vocabulary should focus on:

- Family Members
- Occupations
- Household Objects
- Classroom Objects
- Colors
- Common Adjectives
- Possessive Words
- Everyday Nouns

---

# 📌 Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 02, you should be able to:

- Identify the gender (der, die, das) of common nouns.
- Introduce and describe family members.
- Describe everyday objects using colors and simple adjectives.
- Talk about your home and classroom.
- Use new vocabulary naturally in speaking and writing.
- Recognize all week's vocabulary while reading and listening.

---

# 🔁 Daily Revision Strategy

Every study session should include:

- ✅ Learn 15 new words
- ✅ Revise yesterday's vocabulary
- ✅ Revise older vocabulary from Week 01
- ✅ Speak at least 5 newly learned words
- ✅ Write at least 5 sentences using today's vocabulary
- ✅ Identify noun gender while revising

---

# 🧠 Best Practices

- Always learn nouns together with their article (**der / die / das**).
- Learn vocabulary by theme instead of isolated lists.
- Say every word aloud while learning it.
- Use new words in meaningful sentences.
- Review difficult words more frequently.
- Continue adding every word to your Obsidian Vocabulary Vault.

# 🎯 End of Week Outcome

By the end of Week 02, you should comfortably be able to:

- Talk about your family.
- Name common household and classroom objects.
- Describe objects using colors and simple adjectives.
- Recognize and use the correct articles for common nouns.
- Use approximately **210 active vocabulary words** (Week 01 + Week 02) in simple conversations.

---

> **Remember:** Don't memorize words in isolation. Learn them with their article, pronunciation, and a meaningful sentence. Consistent daily revision is far more effective than learning large lists at once.
