# 🇩🇪 Week 01 — Building Your German Foundation

> **Theme:** _"Hello Germany!"_

Welcome to the first week of your German learning journey.

This week is designed for **absolute beginners** and assumes **no prior knowledge of German**. By the end of the week, you will be able to introduce yourself, greet people, ask simple personal questions, understand very basic conversations, and write a short self-introduction.

Rather than rushing into grammar, Week 1 focuses on building confidence and developing correct pronunciation habits from the very beginning.

---

# 🎯 Weekly Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 1, you should be able to:

- Understand the German alphabet
- Pronounce German letters correctly
- Use personal pronouns

- Form basic statements

# 🏆 Success Criteria

Before moving to Week 2, confirm that you can:

- [ ] Pronounce the German alphabet accurately.
- [ ] Introduce yourself confidently.
- [ ] Ask someone's name.
- [ ] Say where you are from.
- [ ] Count from 0 to 20.
- [ ] Use **ich**, **du**, **er**, **sie**, **es**, **wir**, **ihr**, **Sie** correctly.
- [ ] Conjugate **sein** in the present tense.
- [ ] Read and understand short beginner dialogues.
- [ ] Write a 40–60 word self-introduction.
- [ ] Complete the Week 1 quiz with at least **80% accuracy**.

# 🚀 Looking Ahead

Week 2 will expand these foundations by introducing:

- Definite and indefinite articles
- Noun gender
- Singular and plural forms
- Family vocabulary
- Describing people
- Simple adjective usage
- Everyday conversations

Everything learned in Week 2 builds directly on the concepts introduced this week.

---

> **"Don't aim to speak perfect German this week. Aim to speak German every day."**

Week 1 is about building confidence, forming habits, and laying a solid foundation. Focus on consistent daily practice, clear pronunciation, and understanding rather than speed. A strong start here will make every subsequent week easier.
