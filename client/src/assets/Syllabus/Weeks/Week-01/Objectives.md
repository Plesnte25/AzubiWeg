# 🎯 Week 01 Learning Objectives

> **Mission Statement**
>
> Build a strong foundation in German pronunciation, basic communication, and sentence construction so that you can confidently introduce yourself and understand simple beginner conversations.

---

# 📌 Week Goal

At the end of Week 1 you should no longer feel like you're memorizing random German words.

Instead, you should understand **how basic German sentences work**, recognize common expressions, and confidently participate in your first simple conversation.

---

# 🇪🇺 CEFR Alignment (A1)

This week's objectives align with the official CEFR A1 descriptors.

By the end of the week, you should be able to:

- Understand familiar everyday expressions.
- Introduce yourself.
- Introduce another person.
- Ask and answer simple personal questions.
- Understand slow, carefully spoken German.
- Read short and familiar texts.
- Write a short self-introduction.

---

# 🟥 Grammar Objectives

## Primary Goal

Understand the basic building blocks of German.

### Topics

- German alphabet
- German pronunciation
- Personal pronouns
- Present tense of **sein**
- Basic sentence structure
- W-questions
- Yes/No questions

---

### You should be able to

- [ ] Recognize every German letter.
- [ ] Pronounce umlauts correctly.
- [ ] Pronounce **ß** correctly.
- [ ] Use personal pronouns naturally.
- [ ] Conjugate **sein** without looking.
- [ ] Build simple affirmative sentences.
- [ ] Build simple questions.
- [ ] Recognize correct German word order.

---

### Expected Output

Example sentences such as

- Ich bin Sharjeel.
- Ich komme aus Indien.
- Ich wohne in Patna.
- Wer bist du?
- Wo wohnst du?

# 🟦 Reading Objectives

## Goal

Become comfortable reading simple German text.

---

### Reading Skills

You should be able to

- [ ] Read greetings.
- [ ] Read names.
- [ ] Read countries.
- [ ] Read numbers.
- [ ] Read short conversations.
- [ ] Understand beginner classroom instructions.

---

### Reading Strategy

For every passage

1. Read silently.
2. Read aloud.
3. Highlight unknown words.
4. Answer comprehension questions.
5. Summarize in your own words.

---

### Weekly Reading Outcome

Comfortably understand a simple dialogue between two people meeting for the first time.

---

# 🟩 Listening Objectives

## Goal

Train your ears to recognize German sounds from Day 1.

---

### Listening Skills

You should be able to

- [ ] Recognize alphabet pronunciation.
- [ ] Understand greetings.
- [ ] Identify names.
- [ ] Understand nationalities.
- [ ] Recognize numbers.
- [ ] Follow beginner dialogues.

---

### Listening Strategy

For every audio

1. Listen without subtitles.
2. Listen with subtitles.
3. Repeat after the speaker.
4. Listen again without subtitles.

---

### Weekly Outcome

Understand a slow A1 self-introduction.

---

# 🎖 Week 01 Completion Criteria

You are ready to proceed to **Week 02** when you can confidently:

- ✅ Read simple greetings without assistance.
- ✅ Introduce yourself naturally.
- ✅ Ask and answer basic personal questions.
- ✅ Understand slow beginner conversations.
- ✅ Write a 40–60 word self-introduction.
- ✅ Use **sein** correctly in everyday sentences.
- ✅ Recall most of the week's vocabulary without relying on English.

---

# 🚀 Preparation for Week 02

Week 2 builds directly on this foundation.

You will begin learning:

- Definite articles (**der, die, das**)
- Indefinite articles (**ein, eine**)
- Noun gender
- Singular and plural forms
- Family vocabulary
- Describing people and relationships

Mastering the objectives in Week 1 will make these topics significantly easier.

---

> **"The goal of Week 1 is not to know a lot of German—it is to become comfortable using the German you know."**
