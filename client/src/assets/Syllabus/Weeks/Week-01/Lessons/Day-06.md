# 🟦 Reading

## Reading Exercise

Read a beginner self-introduction from your Nicos Weg lesson or German playlist.

### Tasks

- Read silently.
- Read aloud.
- Highlight unknown words.
- Summarize the passage in **3 simple German sentences**.

---

### Comprehension Questions

Answer in German where possible.

1. What is the person's name?
2. Where are they from?
3. Where do they live?
4. What language do they speak?
5. What is their profession?

---

# 🟩 Listening

## Listening Practice

Use today's beginner dialogue from:

- Nicos Weg
- Your YouTube lesson

### Listening Method

1. Listen without subtitles.
2. Listen with subtitles.
3. Listen again.
4. Repeat every sentence aloud.

---

### Goal

Understand the conversation without translating every word.

# 🟩 Speaking

## Speaking Practice

Without reading, introduce yourself.

Include:

- Greeting
- Name
- Country
- Nationality
- City
- Languages
- Profession
- Farewell

---

### Conversation Practice

Imagine meeting someone for the first time.

Practice asking:

- What's your name?
- Where are you from?
- Where do you live?
- What languages do you speak?

Then answer each question naturally.

---

### Shadowing

Repeat today's dialogue **5 times**, matching pronunciation and rhythm as closely as possible.

---

# 🟪 Writing

- Correct spelling.
- Improve vocabulary where possible.
