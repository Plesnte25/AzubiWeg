# 🟥 Grammar

## Topic

Classroom Expressions & Common Commands

### Learn

Understand the meaning and usage of common classroom instructions such as:

- Lesen Sie.
- Hören Sie zu.
- Schreiben Sie.
- Sprechen Sie.
- Wiederholen Sie.
- Öffnen Sie das Buch.
- Schließen Sie das Buch.
- Arbeiten Sie zusammen.

### Review

- Personal Pronouns
- Verb **sein**
- W-Questions
- Basic Sentence Structure

### Practice

- Read each command aloud.
- Imagine your teacher giving each instruction.
- Repeat until pronunciation feels natural.

# 🟩 Listening

## Topic

Teacher–Student Conversation

Use:

- Nicos Weg lesson
- Your German playlist
- Classroom dialogue audio

### Listening Strategy

1. Listen without subtitles.
2. Listen with subtitles.
3. Repeat after each sentence.
4. Listen once more without subtitles.

---

### Listening Questions

Can you understand:

- Who is speaking?
- What instructions are given?
- What objects are mentioned?
- What questions are asked?
