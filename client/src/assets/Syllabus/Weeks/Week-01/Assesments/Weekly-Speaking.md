# 🗣 Week 01 Speaking Assessment

## Objective

Introduce yourself naturally without reading from notes.

---

## Task 1

Speak for **2–3 minutes**.

Include:

- Greeting
- Name
- Country
- City
- Nationality
- Occupation
- Languages
- Farewell

---

## Task 2

Answer these questions aloud.

- Wie heißt du?
- Woher kommst du?
- Wo wohnst du?
- Wie geht es dir?
- Welche Sprache sprichst du?

---

## Pronunciation Checklist

- [ ] Clear pronunciation
- [ ] Correct verb forms
- [ ] Natural pace
- [ ] Minimal hesitation

---

## Recording

- [ ] Recorded
- [ ] Reviewed
- [ ] Repeated after corrections
