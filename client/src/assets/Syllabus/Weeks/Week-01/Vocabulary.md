# 📖 Week 01 Vocabulary

> **Purpose:** This file serves as the vocabulary guide for Week 01. All detailed vocabulary entries are maintained in your **Obsidian Vocabulary Vault**, while this document defines the themes, learning goals, and revision strategy for the week.

---

# 🎯 Weekly Vocabulary Goal

- **Target:** 105 new words
- **Daily Goal:** 15 new words
- **Revision:** Every day (spaced repetition)

The focus is **not** on memorizing isolated words, but on understanding and using them in context through reading, listening, speaking, and writing.

---

# 📚 Vocabulary Themes

During Week 01, you will learn vocabulary related to:

- Greetings & Farewells
- Introducing Yourself
- Countries & Nationalities
- Numbers (0–20)
- Classroom Expressions
- Common Verbs
- Everyday Nouns
- Basic Question Words

---

# 📌 Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 01, you should be able to:

- Recognize all week's vocabulary in reading and listening.
- Pronounce every word correctly.
- Use new words in simple sentences.
- Recall common words without translating them mentally.
- Apply the vocabulary naturally in basic conversations.

# 🔁 Daily Revision Strategy

Every study session should include:

- ✅ Learn 15 new words
- ✅ Revise yesterday's words
- ✅ Revise older words using spaced repetition
- ✅ Speak at least 5 of the new words
- ✅ Write at least 5 sentences using today's vocabulary

---

# 🧠 Best Practices

- Learn words by **theme**, not randomly.
- Always learn nouns with their article.
- Learn verbs with their common conjugation patterns.
- Use every new word in a sentence.
- Practice pronunciation while learning the meaning.
- Review consistently instead of cramming.
