# 🗺️ Master Curriculum Roadmap

> This document defines the complete learning journey from **Absolute Beginner (A0)** to **Independent User (B1)**.

Unlike traditional study plans, this roadmap follows the **Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR)** while integrating daily practice across the five core language skills.

---

# Curriculum Philosophy

Language learning should be

- Structured
- Progressive
- Practical
- Measurable
- Consistent

Every lesson exists for a reason.

Nothing is studied before its prerequisites.

Every week introduces new concepts while reinforcing previous knowledge through spaced repetition.

# Grammar Roadmap

The grammar progression intentionally follows increasing complexity rather than textbook chapter order.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundations (Weeks 1–8)

Topics

- German Alphabet
- Pronunciation
- Greetings
- Definite Articles
- Indefinite Articles
- Gender
- Singular & Plural
- Personal Pronouns
- Present Tense
- W-Fragen
- Yes/No Questions
- Sentence Structure
- Modal Verbs
- Negation
- Numbers
- Time
- Days & Months
- Accusative Case
- Common Prepositions

Expected Outcome

The learner can communicate in familiar everyday situations.

---

## Phase 2 — Elementary Communication (Weeks 9–17)

Topics

- Perfect Tense
- Dative Case
- Two-way Prepositions
- Reflexive Verbs
- Separable Verbs
- Comparative
- Superlative
- Adjective Endings
- Genitive Introduction
- Subordinate Clauses
- weil
- dass
- wenn
- Future with werden

Expected Outcome

The learner understands connected speech and manages common daily situations independently.

---

## Phase 3 — Independent Communication (Weeks 18–26)

Topics

- Relative Clauses
- Passive Voice
- Konjunktiv II
- Reported Speech
- Advanced Connectors
- Nominalisation
- Advanced Word Order
- Formal Language
- Opinion Structures
- Argumentation

Expected Outcome

The learner can comfortably function at B1 level.

# CEFR Competency Mapping

## A1

The learner can

- Introduce themselves
- Ask simple questions
- Read short texts
- Understand familiar words
- Write basic messages

---

## A2

The learner can

- Describe experiences
- Understand everyday conversations
- Write informal emails
- Read simple articles
- Handle common situations

---

## B1

The learner can

- Express opinions
- Participate in discussions
- Understand authentic texts
- Write formal and informal correspondence
- Communicate independently

---

# Success Criteria

The roadmap is considered complete when the learner can

- [ ] Understand everyday spoken German.
- [ ] Read B1-level texts with confidence.
- [ ] Hold a 10–15 minute conversation on familiar topics.
- [ ] Write formal and informal emails.
- [ ] Understand public announcements and short news reports.
- [ ] Pass a full Goethe/TELC B1 mock examination.

# Final Goal

At the end of Week 26, the learner should possess the knowledge and confidence required to

- Sit for the Goethe Zertifikat B1 examination.
- Sit for the TELC Deutsch B1 examination.
- Participate in Ausbildung interviews.
- Communicate effectively in everyday life in Germany.
- Continue learning independently toward B2.

---

> **Consistency beats intensity.**

This roadmap is designed so that every study session contributes to long-term language acquisition. Follow the sequence, complete the assignments, review regularly, and avoid skipping foundational concepts. Every subsequent week assumes mastery of the previous one.
